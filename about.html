About Adolas Construction Limited

Building Nigeria's dream homes, one site at a time.

We're a Lagos-based construction company that designs and builds housing, estates and luxury residences — with our own engineers and site teams on the ground from first block to final key handover.

Our Story

Started on a site, not in a boardroom.

Adolas Construction Limited (ACL) was founded over a decade ago on a simple idea: that building a family's dream home shouldn't mean gambling their savings on guesswork. What began as a small team taking on individual housing projects has grown into a full construction company trusted with entire estate developments.

We still work the way we started — drawings checked against the wall before it's built, engineers on-site rather than in an office, and one team responsible from the first trench to the final coat of paint. That's how a decade of homes has been delivered without losing the personal accountability our earliest clients signed up for.
